NeuroZoom™ Ingredients

NeuroZoom™ is marketed as a dietary supplement designed to support everyday brain health, memory, focus, and mental performance. Its ingredient profile is described as including vitamins, minerals, botanical extracts, and compounds associated with normal nervous system function.

Key Ingredients in NeuroZoom™

1

Bacopa Monnieri

Bacopa Monnieri is a traditional herb studied for its potential role in memory and learning. Research findings vary depending on the extract, dosage, and duration of use.
2

Green Tea Extract

Green tea extract contains antioxidant plant compounds called catechins. Depending on the formulation, it may also contain caffeine.
3

Choline Bitartrate

Choline is an essential nutrient used by the body to produce acetylcholine, a neurotransmitter involved in memory, learning, and other nervous system functions.
4

Phosphatidylserine

Phosphatidylserine is a phospholipid found in cell membranes, including those of brain cells. It has been studied for its potential relationship with cognitive function.
5

Huperzine A

Huperzine A is a plant-derived compound studied for its effects on acetylcholine breakdown. It can interact with medications, so its use requires caution.
6

DHA (Omega-3 Fatty Acid)

DHA is an omega-3 fatty acid that contributes to the structure of cell membranes, including those in the brain.
7

N-Acetyl-L-Tyrosine

This form of tyrosine is used in some dietary supplements. Tyrosine is involved in the production of certain neurotransmitters.
8

Ginkgo Biloba

Ginkgo Biloba is a botanical ingredient studied for its potential effects on cognitive function and circulation. Evidence of benefits varies, and it may interact with certain medications.
9

Vitamin B6

Vitamin B6 contributes to normal nervous system function and supports the body's production of neurotransmitters.
10

Magnesium

Magnesium is an essential mineral involved in nerve signaling, muscle function, and energy metabolism.
